# Original brand and USP mapping

The original public site was inspected in Chrome before this revision. Its actual logo was downloaded from the first rendered header image. The original site uses black CTAs, white backgrounds, neutral greys and Red Hat Display. These replace the first version’s purple palette and substitute logo.

The original marketing copy is retained in the redesigned hierarchy. Source snapshots with URLs are in `content/`. Product illustration labels are new explanatory UI; profiles, jobs and statuses within the graphics are example data.

| Proposition | Original source | Visual representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI CV builder / CV upload | Candidate page, CV tools | Guided experience/skills/achievements inputs becoming a structured CV |
| AI-assisted shortlisting | Homepage and employer page | Employer dashboard, role context and matched candidate cards |
| Identity verification | Verification & Badges page | Candidate profile with identity status, timestamp label and visible badge |
| UK right-to-work eligibility checks | Explicit client confirmation in this chat | Separate UK eligibility status in the candidate verification graphic |
| Skills, location, pay and availability matching | AI Matching page | Four criteria connected around the original HireHub logo |
| Employer dashboards and workflows | Employer page | Live roles, conversations and closed roles pipeline |
| No recruitment agency commission | Homepage | Zero-percent commission ticket with job-based and subscription options |
| Permanent, temporary and same-day hiring | Homepage and Temp Work page | Availability calendar and three distinct work-type cards |
| Identity, training, profile completion and employer-rating badges | Verification & Badges page | Four distinct badge illustrations |

Registration continues to point to the existing employer/candidate portal routes. Existing editorial placeholders and duplicated headings are not reproduced. Actual portal screens, employer logos, testimonial identities and success figures are not invented.

Validation: structural/link checks for eight pages and browser layout checks at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 pixels, plus registration chooser, pricing/journey tabs and mobile navigation.
